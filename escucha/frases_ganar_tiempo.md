# Frases para ganar tiempo en una llamada (contenido del skill /escucha, no código)

Lo lee `escucha/aviso_compact.py`: una sección `## <situacion>` por situación, una frase por
línea que empiece con `- `. Se eligen 2-3 al azar de la sección pedida; si la sección no existe
se usa `## general`. Editar aquí cambia lo que Bernard ve en la siguiente llamada, sin tocar código.
Las frases son cortas, en inglés, en primera persona, y ninguna admite que hay una IA detrás.

## compactando
- Give me a second, I'm taking a note of that.
- Hold on one moment, let me write that down.
- Let me double-check that on my side real quick.
- One sec, I'm catching up with my notes.

## buscando-en-codigo
- Let me look that up in the code real quick.
- Give me a moment, I want to find where that lives.
- Hold on, I'm checking the repository for that.
- Let me pull that file up on my side.

## esperando-build
- The build is still running on my side, give it a few seconds.
- It's compiling, should be done in a moment.
- Still waiting on the build, almost there.
- Let me wait for that to finish before I move on.

## no-entendi
- Sorry, can you say that last part again?
- I missed that, could you repeat the last step?
- Which menu was that, sorry?
- Let me make sure I got that right: you said...?

## tarea-larga
- This will take me a minute, bear with me.
- Let me finish this step, it's a bit slow on my end.
- Give me a moment, I'm working through it.
- Almost there, just a few more seconds.

## general
- Give me a second.
- One moment, please.
- Let me check that.
